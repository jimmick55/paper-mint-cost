Cost is the winning leg's haircut: 0.2 bp deadband, then the impact curve (baseRate 10%, rateMultiplier 15,000, referenceNotional $100k, positionMultiplier 483.979 ETH / 814.598 BTC), then a 2% win fee. It matched real ETH pairs to 0.1%. Losing legs pay nothing extra. Papertrade docs "$/1k after wait" assumes the rate keeps falling at your measured speed for the average time price took to make that move. The recommended target is the move with the lowest such cost, never under 0.5%, and at least 0.15% inside liquidation. Liquidation fires about 5 bps before zero equity and takes the full margin.
