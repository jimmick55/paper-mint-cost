Cost per 1,000 PAPER by the move you wait for

The Hyperliquid hedge pays a fixed fee per dollar of notional, so it gets cheaper as the move grows. Both-on-Papertrade pays a share of the winning leg's gain, which never drops below about 12%, so it wins only on small moves.

Capital and liquidation

Campaign total

Papertrade: no open/close fee, no funding; mint basis is 98% of the loss while the queue is empty; liquidation fires about 5 bps before zero equity and forfeits the full margin. Winning closes lose a 0.2 bp deadband, then an impact haircut (baseRate 10%, rateMultiplier 15,000, referenceNotional $100k, positionMultiplier 814.598 BTC / 483.979 ETH), then a 2% win fee. Papertrade docs Hyperliquid: perps fee tiers and HYPE staking discounts from the fee schedule. Taker fills pay half the spread versus Papertrade's mid. Max leverage BTC 40×, ETH 25×. The mint rate falls as you mint: your own losses advance the LP high-water mark, so PAPER per cycle is integrated along the curve. Not financial advice.
